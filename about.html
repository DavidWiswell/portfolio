About

Comedy grounded in human behavior.

American writer based in São Paulo creating comedy and dramedy projects about outsiders, ambition, cultural collision, social friction, and the strange social theater of modern life.

Background

I previously wrote a weekly humor column on American politics for Folha de S.Paulo and have developed comedy across stand-up, sketch, digital media, television, and hosted satire, including work viewed by millions online.

Creative Focus

Across scripted and unscripted formats, my work explores performance, ambition, and what people become when success requires becoming someone else.
