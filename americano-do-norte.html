Featured Project

Americano do Norte

Half-Hour Workplace ComedySocial Satire · Ensemble Comedy

When an America-obsessed Brazilian executive hires a directionless American as a symbol of international prestige, the hapless outsider becomes trapped inside a workplace where everyone is secretly improvising competence.

Tone

A fast-paced ensemble workplace comedy blending sharp social satire, cringe humor, and emotional realism. Exploring social norms, class anxieties, and the aspirational culture of modern São Paulo through the lens of a well-intentioned American, who himself is way over his head. Awkward, chaotic, character-driven and grounded in genuine humanity.

Why You'll Be Hooked

Hooking audiences with the familiar appeal of a workplace comedy seen through an entirely new cultural lens, the chaos of its fish-out-of-water premise gradually reveals a surprisingly human story about ambition, compromise, and the uncomfortable question of whether integrity is a luxury only some people can afford.
