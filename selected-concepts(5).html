Expanded Slate

Selected Concepts

Alongside my featured projects, I continually develop original concepts across film and television. Most begin with a simple ironic premise before expanding into character-driven stories with strong commercial hooks.

Conspiracy What if every insane conspiracy theorist was right?

After his revolutionary discovery transforms him from scientific outcast to the world's most celebrated scientist, an uncompromising empiricist uncovers evidence that every absurd conspiracy theory is actually true. Cast out once again, he reluctantly joins forces with the internet's most notorious conspiracy theorist to expose the truth—until one final discovery forces him to dismantle the conspiracy movement he unleashed in a world where he can no longer trust anyone, not even himself.

Armchair President The loudest critic suddenly has to be president.

After his mega-viral election-night video destroys both major presidential candidates, a know-it-all political YouTuber is elected President in an unprecedented write-in campaign. Now he must discover that running the country is a lot harder than running his mouth—before he destroys it.

The Petty Brothers PR gurus take on people’s petty grievances.

After rising young rivals use their own PR playbook to destroy their reputations, two legendary, yet immature, PR fixers are forced to reinvent themselves by launching the world's first micro-PR app, using elite crisis-management tactics to solve the hilariously petty personal scandals of ordinary people while clawing their way back to the top of the industry.

Super Ego When a pretender has to become the real thing.

After the world's most narcissistic superhero star gains actual superpowers, he must become the hero he's always pretended to be when the director whose career he destroyed gains godlike abilities and becomes convinced the only way to save humanity is to destroy the society that rewards monsters.

Super Down The world’s greatest hero has lost the will to save it.

After learning the devastating truth about his own origin leaves him crippled by depression, the world's greatest superhero must find the strength to fight when every supervillain he ever defeated escapes at once and threatens to destroy the world.

Rebel Without a Santa Claus Disillusionment is part of growing up.

After a viral moment turns a shy orphan into an overnight child star, he discovers that Santa Claus isn't real while filming a Christmas movie, sending him on a mission to reveal the truth to every child on Earth—until he uncovers an even greater secret: the real Santa has been kidnapped by the very forces manipulating him to replace the world's wonder with consumerism.

Stay To save another you must first save yourself.

After becoming separated from his seven-year-old autistic owner during a trip for experimental treatment, a devoted emotional support dog is captured by the abusive family he escaped years earlier and must find his way back to save the only person he's ever truly loved before she’s lost forever in a strange city.

Life Writes The people he betrayed now own his future.

After becoming a literary superstar by exploiting the darkest secrets of his friends and family, a shameless writer must somehow convince the people who hate him most to sign away their life rights before a massive class-action lawsuit costs him everything.

Seize the Day He has one month left to settle the score.

After a terminal cancer diagnosis gives him one month to live, a bitter kindergarten teacher decides there's one more thing he must do before dying—get revenge on his estranged backstabbing brother who ruined his life—only to discover dying is easier than letting go.

Paine The father of modern democracy died forgotten.

After a self-educated English corset maker inspires the American and French Revolutions with a series of world-changing ideas, his uncompromising belief in universal human rights turns yesterday's hero into today's traitor, leaving the father of modern democracy exiled by every nation he helped transform.

Beneath the Surface Who traded our wants for our needs?

The untold story of Edward Bernays, Sigmund Freud's nephew, whose revolutionary application of psychology to advertising and politics replaced need with hidden desire, creating the modern consumer, toppling governments, and opening a Pandora's box of mass persuasion that transformed an ordinary publicist into the unseen architect of the modern world.

Invasion of the Basic Bitch Body Snatchers Original thought is humanity’s last line of defense.

When an offbeat painter discovers that painfully ordinary people are actually body-snatching creatures who use excruciatingly dull small talk to blend into society, he must expose the impostors that have infiltrated his artists' retreat before they assimilate the last people on Earth with an original thought.

Red Christmas Santa finally snaps.

After centuries of bearing the burden of humanity's darkest secrets, Santa Claus finally snaps and embarks on a bloody crusade to purge the world of its naughty—turning Christmas into the deadliest night of the year.

“He knows if you’ve been bad or good… and he’s fucking had enough.”

See No Evil Hearing his greatest mistake getting closer.

When a purist painter is blinded in an accident, he secludes himself in an abandoned Buddhist sanctuary to master life through his other senses. But when the embittered former protégé whose career he destroyed tracks him down, he must use everything he's learned to survive an enemy he can no longer see.

Ultimate Survivalist Every alliance has its expiration date.

Ten elite survivalists are dropped into the wilderness with one randomly assigned survival item each, forcing them into uneasy alliances for food, fire, and shelter—even though every alliance must eventually be abandoned if they're to become the last survivalist standing.

He’s the Boss The toughest TV exec is seven years old.

A comedy team is tasked with creating a television pilot from scratch, but there's one catch: their executive producer is seven years old and has final say over every step of the process. From the script and casting to set design and the final edit, every decision must be greenlit by their young boss—and every absurd note must be incorporated.

I’m with Stupid Every expert starts as a complete idiot.

A quick-witted but hopelessly unqualified comedian immerses himself in some of the world's toughest jobs, cultures, and ways of life, where he must do everything himself—with guidance, but no help. Whether he's surviving in the wilderness, enduring military boot camp, living with an Indigenous community, or training like an astronaut, he'll gripe, panic, embarrass himself, and occasionally succeed while gaining a newfound respect for people living lives completely unlike his own.
