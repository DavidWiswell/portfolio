Featured Project

Date My Daughter

Hidden-Camera Dating Comedy FormatCringe Comedy · Social Experimentation

Logline

A hidden-camera dating show where unsuspecting men are secretly put through emotional, moral, and social tests, while their date’s father watches and manipulates everything from the control room.

Tone

A mix of hidden-camera chaos, cringe comedy, and emotionally grounded social experimentation — combining the escalating discomfort of Impractical Jokers with the emotional volatility of modern dating culture. Funny, unpredictable, awkward, heartfelt — and highly clip-able.

Why This Story

A scalable hidden-camera social experiment format built for viral clips, emotional pressure, and endlessly adaptable cultural dynamics.
