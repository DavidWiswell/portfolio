Featured Project

Gringo in Paradise

Half-Hour Dark Comedy / DramedySatirical · Emotional · Chaotic

Logline

After destroying their careers in America, a self-sabotaging comedian and his embittered ex must sustain an escalating lie that he’s a famous U.S. celebrity while hosting a Brazilian travel series that’s become their last shot at redemption.

Tone

A dark, fast-paced comedy blending sharp satire, emotional realism, and escalating deception. The series combines the self-destructive character comedy of BoJack Horseman with the tension, reinvention, and moral improvisation of Better Call Saul — set against a seductive fantasy of starting again in contemporary Brazil.

Why This Story

A dark comedy about identity fraud, addiction, reinvention, and the blurred line between performance and authenticity. Through dark comedy, cultural collision, and emotional sabotage, the series examines addiction, status, loneliness and our core struggle for identity.
